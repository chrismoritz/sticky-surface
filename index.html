Overview

An entirely new kind of grand tourer

Aurelia GT pairs a 620-mile dual-motor platform with a cabin tuned for long-distance calm. Every surface, every sound, every input has been reconsidered around a single idea: the car should disappear so the journey doesn't have to.

Design

Shaped by air, not trend

A single unbroken silhouette, flush glass, and a drag coefficient of 0.198 — the lowest in its class. Nothing on Aurelia GT's exterior exists without a reason.

Interior

A cabin tuned like an instrument

Hand-finished open-pore wood, 22-way adaptive seating, and acoustic glass that cuts outside noise by 40%. Interior Features showcases every material choice in detail.

Technology

Software that learns the road

DriveSense™ Assist reads traffic patterns a quarter-mile ahead. Over-the-air updates mean Aurelia GT gets quicker, quieter, and smarter after you've bought it.

Performance

620 horsepower, delivered quietly

Dual permanent-magnet motors and a low-slung 97kWh pack give Aurelia GT the balance of a sports sedan with the composure of a flagship. View Specs for the full performance breakdown.
