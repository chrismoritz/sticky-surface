Technology

Software that learns the road

DriveSense™ Assist reads traffic patterns a quarter-mile ahead. Over-the-air updates mean Aurelia GT gets quicker, quieter, and smarter after you've bought it.

Discover what DriveSense sees for you

DriveSense™ Assist

It reads the road a quarter-mile ahead.

Long-range radar, eight cameras and live traffic data work together to anticipate what is coming, then smooth your speed and lane position before you would notice anything changed.

0.25 mi look-ahead

Sees further than you can

Slowdowns, merges and lane closures are picked up a quarter-mile out, so braking is gradual instead of sudden.

8 cameras, plus radar

Learns your commute

On routes you drive often, DriveSense remembers where traffic bunches up and starts easing off a little earlier each time.

30% less phantom braking

Better after you buy it

Updates arrive overnight. The latest added lane-change assist on divided highways and cut unnecessary braking by 30%.

Performance

620 hp, delivered quietly.

Two permanent-magnet motors and a low-slung battery give Aurelia GT the balance of a sports sedan with the composure of a flagship.

2.9s 0–60 mph

Dual motors, one feel

Torque shifts between axles thousands of times a second, so power arrives smoothly whether the road is dry, wet or winding.

50/50 weight balance

Weight where it helps

The 97 kWh pack sits below the floor, for a low center of gravity and an even 50/50 balance front to rear.

620 mi estimated range

Range without compromise

An efficient heat pump and a 0.198 drag coefficient keep 620 miles of estimated range, even with this much power on tap.
